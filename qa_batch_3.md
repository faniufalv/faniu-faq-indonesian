## Q1: [Cross-border E-commerce] What are the new regulations on registered capital under the new Company Law?

**A**: The 2024 revised Company Law: 1) LLC shareholders must fully pay subscribed capital within 5 years from establishment; 2) Joint-stock company promoters must pay upon establishment; 3) State Council may set special provisions. Companies should evaluate capital payment plans promptly.

**法律依据**: 《公司登记管理条例》

**领域**: 新公司法 | **语言**: English

## Q2: [Family Law] How to apply for disability assessment after a traffic accident?

**A**: Traffic accident disability assessment process: 1) Apply to qualified judicial appraisal institution after treatment completion; 2) Submit accident report, medical records, imaging materials; 3) Institution issues assessment within 30 working days; 4) Dissatisfied parties can request re-assessment.

**法律依据**: 《民法典》侵权责任编

**领域**: 交通事故 | **语言**: English
